Bivariate Grid Overlay for two uncertainty dimensions

Synthetic FIRMS-like fire detections

Layers

Distributional uncertainty (JSD): low → high
Structural uncertainty: under → over
Lens bars: FRP of the sample points in the cell, % by class low (< 10 MW) medium (10–40) high (> 40)

Drag the hexagonal lens to another cell (or click a cell): it snaps to the cell, which is spotlighted. The lower band shows the FRP distribution of the sample points in that cell as % of its points, in the classes Low, Medium and High. Click a band to cycle the lens size (1×, 1.5×, 0.75×); with “Lens scales with grid” on it follows the grid cells as you zoom. Use the layer buttons to show or hide sample points, reference points and the uncertainty overlay.
